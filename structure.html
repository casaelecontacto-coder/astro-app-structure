Shared things used on many screens

Term meaning cardMust
A bottom sheet from any underlined word, with “See it in my chart”. Used on Today, Panchang, Chart and People.
Paywall sheetMust
Opens from any paid item. Always closable, returns to the item. One plan, welcome offer for new users.
Audio mini-playerMust
Starts on Today, persists across tabs, can be paused anywhere.
“Likely” labelsMust
When birth time is unknown, time-dependent results carry a “likely” tag. Editing birth details clears it.
Offline & low-data stateMust
Chart and last daily message stay readable. A quiet banner shows when content is cached.
