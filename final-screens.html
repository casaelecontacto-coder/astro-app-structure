Today

The home tab, opened every morning: the day's horoscope as a page with audio read-aloud and the affirmation that also appears on the home screen widget. The streak chip is the way into the Energy streak. Career, love, health and money, the Life phase card and the Week’s overview sit below the first screen. Panchang

One day card with a front and a back. The front names the day and shows the good times. The back opens with the sun arc, then the five limbs in plain words, with the Moon transit and a "For you" line inside Nakshatra.

All names, dates and times on these screens are sample data, not real Panchang values.
