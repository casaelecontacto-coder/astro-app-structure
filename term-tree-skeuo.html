Content rules

Short rules that keep 190-plus cards consistent. They build on the heavy-topic care handling in the plan.

Plain first, Sanskrit second

Write the plain meaning, then place the classical word beside it. Never define a term using another unexplained term.

Care-marked terms are softened

Terms traditionally read as difficult (such as Chandrashtama, Vishti, 8th house) describe what the period is for, not what to fear. No remedies are sold.

Templates for large sets

Yoga, Karana, Tithi and Nakshatra cards share a fixed shape, so an astrologer reviews content rather than structure.

Cards work offline

All cards ship with the app, so tapping a term never needs a connection. Only the “For you” line is calculated.

One owner for each fact

A fact (for example, that Rohini is ruled by the Moon) lives in one place and is reused by every card that mentions it.
